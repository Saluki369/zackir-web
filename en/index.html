It's this easy

Easiest: install nothing. Tap "Open in your browser now" above – done, you can start right away (phone and computer). Tip: in your browser menu choose "Add to home screen" and ZACKIR sits on your phone like an app.

Or install it as an Android app:

  1. Tap "Download Android app (APK)" and wait until the download has finished.
  2. Open the finished file zackir.apk (tap the notification at the top, or use the "Files" / "Downloads" app).
  3. If Android asks: tap "Allow installation from this source", switch it on, then go back.
  4. If "Play Protect" warns you: tap "Install anyway".
  5. Tap "Install" – then "Open". Done.

Download not working? Just use the browser version above. It always works.
